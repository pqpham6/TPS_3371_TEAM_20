# Stakeholders

## Primary Stakeholders

### 1. Referring Provider
The physician, nurse practitioner, or clinician who identifies that a patient needs a service outside their own scope and submits the referral. They need to know their referral was received and track its progress.

### 2. Patient
The individual being referred for care. Patients are directly affected by how quickly and accurately their referral is processed, since delays can impact their access to needed treatment.

### 3. Receiving Department / Specialist Provider
The department or specialist who receives the referral, reviews the submitted information, and decides whether to accept, reject, or request more information before scheduling the patient.

### 4. Referral Coordinator / Administrative Staff
Staff responsible for monitoring incoming referrals, ensuring required information is complete, routing referrals that need manual intervention, and following up on referrals that are stalled or flagged.

## Secondary Stakeholders

### 5. Scheduling Staff
Staff responsible for booking the patient's appointment once a referral has been accepted by the receiving department.

### 6. Healthcare Organization / Clinic Administration
The organization operating the system, interested in reducing administrative overhead, improving patient satisfaction, and maintaining compliance with healthcare data regulations.

### 7. System Administrator
Responsible for managing user accounts, department/service routing rules, and system configuration.

## Stakeholder Interests Summary

| Stakeholder | Primary Interest |
|---|---|
| Referring Provider | Fast, transparent referral submission and status tracking |
| Patient | Timely access to the referred service |
| Receiving Department | Clear, complete referral information to act on |
| Referral Coordinator | Ability to monitor, route, and resolve incomplete/stalled referrals |
| Scheduling Staff | Accurate, accepted referrals ready to be scheduled |
| Clinic Administration | Reduced administrative cost, improved compliance, better outcomes |
| System Administrator | Manageable, configurable, secure system |
