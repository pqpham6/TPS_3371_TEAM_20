# Business Problem

## Overview
Healthcare providers routinely need to refer patients to other departments, specialists, or external services for care that falls outside their own scope of practice. Today, this referral process at many clinics and healthcare networks is handled manually or through disconnected systems (phone calls, faxes, paper forms, or unstructured emails). This leads to a number of persistent problems:

- Referrals are frequently **lost or delayed** because there is no centralized system to track them from submission to completion.
- Referring providers have **no visibility** into whether a referral was received, is under review, has been accepted, or has been rejected.
- Referrals are sometimes submitted **without complete information** (e.g., missing diagnosis codes, insurance details, or urgency level), causing back-and-forth delays between the referring and receiving departments.
- There is no standardized way to **route** a referral to the correct department or specialist based on the service requested.
- Patients experience **delays in receiving care** because referrals sit unprocessed or fall through administrative cracks.
- Administrative staff spend significant time manually following up on referral status via phone or email.

## The Problem Statement
There is no unified system that allows a referring provider to submit a patient referral, have it automatically validated and routed to the correct receiving department, and have all parties track its status in real time until the patient is scheduled or the referral is resolved.

## Why This Matters
Delayed or mismanaged referrals can directly affect patient outcomes, especially for time-sensitive specialty care. A centralized Patient Referral Management system reduces administrative overhead, improves communication between referring and receiving providers, and gives patients and staff clear visibility into where a referral stands at any given time.

## Proposed Solution
Our team will design and build a **Patient Referral Management System** that allows:
- Referring providers to submit structured, complete referrals for a patient to receive a specific service.
- The system to validate required information before a referral is accepted into the workflow.
- Automatic routing of the referral to the appropriate receiving department based on the requested service.
- Tracking of the referral through its full lifecycle (submitted → under review → accepted/rejected → scheduled → completed).
- Visibility into referral status for referring providers, receiving departments, and administrative staff.
